01 · About

About me

I’m a Computer Systems Technician graduate interested in IT support, systems administration, and identity and access management. I like understanding how systems work, why they fail, and how to make the experience easier for the people using them.

[01] BACKGROUND

Where I’m coming from

My training at Seneca Polytechnic gave me hands-on experience with Windows Server, Linux, Microsoft Azure, networking, virtualization, scripting, and enterprise support. I learned by building environments, breaking them, troubleshooting them, and documenting what I changed.

My co-op experience added the part that labs cannot fully recreate: supporting real users while systems are actively being used. That meant balancing technical troubleshooting with communication, urgency, and making sure the user could get back to work.

[02] INTERESTS

What I like working on

01

Identity & access

User lifecycle tasks, password and account recovery, MFA, permissions, RBAC, least privilege, and understanding how access should be managed safely.

02

IT support

Troubleshooting Windows, Microsoft 365, VPN, account, endpoint, and connectivity issues while keeping the user informed.

03

Infrastructure

Networking, Windows Server, Linux, virtualization, cloud services, and the systems that sit behind everyday business operations.

04

Documentation

Turning technical work into clear notes, repeatable steps, and explanations that another person can actually use.

[03] APPROACH

Curious by default.

I try not to stop at “it works.” I want to know what caused the issue, what fixed it, and whether the solution is reliable enough to repeat. When I troubleshoot, I prefer to work from the simplest explanation outward instead of changing several things at once.

I also care about making technical work understandable. A solution is more useful when it can be explained, documented, and supported by someone other than the person who built it.

[04] NOW

What I’m building toward

I’m continuing to strengthen my Microsoft 365, Active Directory, Entra ID, Intune, networking, and IAM knowledge while building a portfolio that shows the work behind the skills on my resume.

I’m especially interested in opportunities where I can keep developing through practical support work: Help Desk, Service Desk, Desktop Support, IT Support, and IAM-adjacent roles.
