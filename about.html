About me

I’m Sidoney, a Computer Systems Technician graduate who likes understanding why something stopped working, what fixed it, and how to make the solution easier to repeat next time. IT is a good fit for me because there is always another system, problem, or idea to figure out.

[01] BACKGROUND

Where I’m coming from

My Computer Systems Technician program at Seneca Polytechnic gave me hands-on experience with Windows Server, Linux, Azure, networking, virtualization, and scripting. The part I enjoyed most was practical: build the environment, test it, break something, trace the problem, and understand why the fix worked.

My Junior Systems Administrator co-op added the part that labs cannot fully recreate: the issue belongs to a real user who needs to get back to work. Supporting accounts, MFA, VPN access, Microsoft 365, endpoints, and onboarding/offboarding taught me to balance technical troubleshooting with clear communication and useful documentation.

[03] APPROACH

Curious by default.

I try not to stop at “it works.” I want to know what caused the issue, what fixed it, and whether the solution is reliable enough to repeat. When I troubleshoot, I prefer to work from the simplest explanation outward instead of changing several things at once.

I also care about making technical work understandable. A solution is more useful when it can be explained, documented, and supported by someone other than the person who built it.
